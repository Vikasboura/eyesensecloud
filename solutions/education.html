Challenges We Solve

Ensure student safety and prevent security failures inside school campuses.

Attendance Fraud

Proxy signatures, manual record errors, and delayed reporting of absent students to guardians.

Student Safety Vulnerabilities

Unmonitored transit buses, student location blind spots, and lack of real-time transit logs.

Perimeter Violations

Trespassing, unregistered visitor entries, and gate security breakdowns during pick-up/drop-off hours.

Crisis Management Failures

Absence of one-click emergency broadcast triggers, and delayed evacuation warnings.

EyeSense Solution for Education Sector

A comprehensive safety platform to monitor, notify, and protect school children.

Facial Attendance

Instant contactless attendance scanners at gates or classroom doors, automatically notifying parents of late-comings.

Smart Bus Tracking

Live bus GPS coordinates, board/deboard student notifications, and route speed violation alerts for administration.

Visitor Authentication

Parent QR gate check-ins, one-time verification codes for authorized guardians, and restricted blacklist alerts.

Perimeter AI Shield

Automatic detection of boundary scaling, loitering, and unidentified vehicles in student zones.

One-touch SOS triggers sent directly to security personnel, local emergency services, and parents with live audio feed.

Health & Hygiene Alerts

Thermal scanners at entry gates to monitor body temperatures and alert on potential contamination spreads.
