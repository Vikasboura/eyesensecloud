Why EyeSense & ENTELI-PRO?

Traditional business and security management systems have historically operated in isolated silos. Surveillance was a passive record investigated only after an incident occurred, while daily administration relied on fragmented spreadsheets and manual registers.

EyeSense with ENTELI-PRO changes this paradigm. By uniting real-time AI computer vision with cloud-native business workflows, we convert camera feeds into automated intelligence while synchronizing tenant management, staff administration, and billing into one synchronized ecosystem.

Built to serve businesses across diverse industries, ENTELI-PRO aims to make world-class business technology accessible to everyone—empowering organizations to simplify operations, stay connected, and grow with confidence.

Our Foundational Standards

  • Privacy Centric: Local anonymization on biometric data and license plates protects identity with enterprise security.
  • Zero Hardware Lock-In: Effortlessly connects with standard IP cameras, NVRs, mobile devices, and existing office infrastructure.
  • Sub-2s Real-Time Triggers: Instant communications, guard alerts, and management notices under 2 seconds of threat validation.
  • Scalable Cloud Architecture: Multi-tenant, reliable infrastructure that scales effortlessly from single buildings to multi-city operations.
